Allowance

Give your AI agent a budget, not your card.

1. Issue a spending mandate

A mandate is the only authority the agent gets: a goal, a cap, and a window. It cannot spend a cent outside it.

2. Run the agent

The agent plans searches, queries the live Channel3 catalog, and picks one option with its reasoning. It cannot buy anything yet.
