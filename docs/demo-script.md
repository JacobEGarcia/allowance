# Demo video script (< 3 min, screen recording, no face needed)

Target: 2:15. Record at 1080p, browser fullscreen on the hosted demo.

1. (0:00-0:15) Cold open on the landing view. VO/text card:
   "AI agents are starting to shop for us. Today the only safe way to let one
   pay is to not let it pay at all. Allowance is the middle path."

2. (0:15-0:45) Issue a mandate: goal "a warm desk lamp for late-night reading",
   cap $50, 7 days. Point at the mandate card:
   "This mandate is the only authority the agent gets. A goal, a cap, a window.
   No card details, no open wallet."

3. (0:45-1:30) Run the agent (live keys, not demo mode). Narrate the log as it
   appears: "The LLM plans two searches against the Channel3 catalog - live
   products, live prices - then ranks the in-budget candidates and picks one,
   with its reasoning."

4. (1:30-2:00) Approval gate: "And here is the whole point - it cannot buy
   anything. I approve the exact item and the exact price." Click Approve.
   PayPal sandbox checkout: "Payment runs through PayPal's sandbox Orders flow,
   locked to the approved amount."

5. (2:00-2:15) Ledger + mandate card now showing reduced budget:
   "Every dollar is written to a ledger with its PayPal order ID, and the
   remaining budget shrinks. Give your agent an allowance - not your card."

End card: repo URL + "PayPal AI Hackathon 2026".
